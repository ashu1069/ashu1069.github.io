About Me

AI Researcher & Engineer | Passionate about Computer Vision & Generative AI

My Journey

Hi! I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Rochester Institute of Technology, NY, USA, with an expected graduation in December 2025. My academic foundation was built at IIT Roorkee, where I completed my Bachelor's in Engineering Physics in 2023. This unique combination of physics and AI has given me a distinctive perspective on solving complex problems.

As an MLE Intern at Owl Autonomous Imaging, I'm diving deep into the complex and challenging world of autonomous driving software system stack. My work encompasses the entire software stack, from sensor integration to perception systems. What excites me most is working on multimodal fusion - combining data from various sensors like RGB-Thermal cameras, LiDAR, and IMU to create robust perception systems. I just love working on critical system design of autonomous vehicles, creating scenarios and figuring out innovative solutions to solve them.

My research interests span multiple domains. For my master's thesis, I'm developing a multicriteria decision analysis framework for global vaccine proposal prioritization. I'm also passionate about exploring cognitive science-inspired generative AI and vision-language models for sports applications. What drives me is not just building models, but creating solutions that can make a real impact - whether it's advancing technology for societal benefit or driving business innovation.

Beyond academics and work, I'm an avid reader who enjoys exploring books across various genres. When I'm not immersed in technology, you'll find me on the cricket field or badminton court, where I channel my competitive spirit. For me, work is my hobby and hobby is my work - I find joy in both the technical challenges of AI and the personal growth that comes from diverse interests.
